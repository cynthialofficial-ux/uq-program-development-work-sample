# UQ Program Development Analysis Work Sample

Independent recruitment work sample prepared by Yaxiya (Cynthia) Li for the Senior Program Development Analyst role in UQ Planning & Business Intelligence (R-69143).

The work sample is structured as practical analyst working material rather than a generic consulting case. It shows how I would:

- frame a program-development decision;
- separate public evidence from internal data requirements and assumptions;
- review internal and external comparators;
- specify the data pull before modelling;
- test applications, commencements, EFTSL/load, retention, pricing and viability; and
- turn the analysis into a concise decision note and stakeholder workplan.

The illustrative program concept is not a UQ proposal. No UQ internal application, load, pricing, cost or business-case data is used. Synthetic values are labelled as such.

## Relevant background

My transferable experience includes enterprise financial and portfolio analysis at Rio Tinto, scenario modelling, data reconciliation, executive decision support, research/survey analysis and cross-functional stakeholder facilitation. I completed a UQ Master of Business in Information Systems and Marketing and returned to UQ Business School as keynote speaker for the 2025 Postgraduate Networking Event.
